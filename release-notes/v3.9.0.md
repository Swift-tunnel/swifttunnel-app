## What's changed

A big update: a cleaner look, a new Setup, and an app that stays out of your game's way while you play.

- New look across the app: flatter panels, new icons, country flags, and a Connect page that shows your session and Roblox at a glance.
- SwiftTunnel's window goes almost idle while you are connected, so it stops taking CPU and GPU time from your game.
- You pick the region and SwiftTunnel picks the relay, skipping crowded ones. Pinning a single server is gone.
- New Auto mode picks the route for every game you join.
- New Setup: one download installs SwiftTunnel or SwiftTunnel Lite, and repairs, reinstalls or removes them later.
- FPS unlock is on by default, and your FFlags are put back by themselves after Roblox updates.
- Fixed connecting failing after 90 seconds on PCs where Windows is slow to respond.

### Interface

- The app opens straight on Connect, behind a loading bar that follows real startup. The Home page is gone.
- Games opens straight on the Roblox settings.
- The live connection graph is off by default. Turn it back on from Connect or Settings.
- The close button hides the window right away, even while connecting.

### Roblox

- New "Reset Roblox to default" button on the Roblox page. It closes Roblox and removes every FFlag on your PC, including ones done by strapper.
- Uninstalling now properly undoes everything SwiftTunnel changed in Roblox. It could previously leave flags and a lowered graphics quality behind.
- Ultraboost now says what it costs: maximum FPS at the lowest detail.
- Removed the NVIDIA Profile Inspector integration.

### Optimize

- Every tweak saves how to undo itself before it changes anything.
- Tweaks that need a PC restart are labelled "Restart PC".
- Presets and bulk actions no longer report success when only part of a change applied.

### Network

- The server list refreshes before every connect, so new and removed locations show up right away.
- Disconnect only reports success once the tunnel is really down.
- Diagnostics and the speed test no longer skew each other's results.

### Setup and updates

- Setup is also available for Windows on ARM.
- Missing installer files are recovered during install, update and uninstall.
- Updates wait for Windows Installer to finish before reporting success.
- Setup asks before restarting Windows instead of restarting by itself.

### Fixes

- The network driver is no longer reinstalled on every other launch.
- The in-game FPS counter no longer drops to 0 while connected.
- Auto-clean RAM leaves SwiftTunnel's own window alone.
- SwiftTunnel Lite starts on PCs without the Visual C++ runtime, and picks up refilled free time without a restart.
- Logs stay small during long sessions.
- Many more small fixes.
