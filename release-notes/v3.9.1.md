## What's changed

- You can choose a specific relay within a region again in Desktop and Lite. Leave it on Automatic to let SwiftTunnel choose.
- The relay picker shows each server's measured ping. Manual selection stays on that relay for the connection.
- If a saved relay is removed or its address changes, SwiftTunnel asks you to choose another relay or Automatic.
