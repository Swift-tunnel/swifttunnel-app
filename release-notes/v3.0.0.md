## What's changed

The biggest release so far: the whole interface has been rebuilt, the app speaks 47 languages, and the relay fleet is back up at 10 servers across 5 regions.

### Highlights

- Every screen redesigned around one telemetry-style design system, so the app reads like a single instrument rather than eight separate panels.
- 47 languages, picked automatically from your Windows locale and switchable any time from the title bar.
- Search everything with `Ctrl+K` - one palette jumps to any tab, setting, or individual tweak across ~50 destinations.
- 65 reversible Windows tweaks split into Game Boost and Speed Up, every one undoable from a single button.
- 10 relays across 5 regions - Mumbai, Singapore, Tokyo, Germany and US East (for now, will be increased later)

### Interface

- Home is new: a live status card, one-tap presets, and your total tunnelled time at a glance.
- Connect now shows a route diagram of you → relay → Roblox, a live throughput graph, and real relay latency instead of a static label.
- Regions groups every relay by city, showing each region's ping and letting you pin a specific server or leave it on Auto.
- Optimize was rebuilt, new tab Speed Up was introduced with tiers, live system-memory readout.
- Games and Boost gained an FPS slider and per-game handling instead of one global setting.
- In-Game overlay was reworked with a contained preview and clearer enable state.
- Diagnostics and Repair were reorganised so the checks that matter are visible without scrolling.
- Settings was regrouped and every option is now reachable from search.

### Presets

- Save your whole configuration as a preset and switch between Low End, Balanced and High End in one click.
- Presets can be exported and imported, so you can share your setup to a friend.

### Network

- Route Assist returns to picking the fastest relay for every match, with the login and matchmaking traffic relayed too.
- Region control-plane hosts now resolve to the tunnelled region's edge, so you land in the region you actually selected instead of your home one.
- Relay latency improved.
- Busy relays are avoided automatically, and you get told if a server you pinned is crowded.

### Free tier

- Everyone gets 3 hours of relay time per day, shown as a live countdown in the title bar and refilling on a rolling 24-hour window.
- This limit is temporary while the fleet scales up and will be removed soon.

### Fixes

- Windows notifications now say SwiftTunnel instead of "Windows PowerShell".
- The live ping reading no longer blanks to a dash mid-session.
- A region shows one ping figure rather than a live number next to a stale one.
- Sign-in no longer fails behind corporate or antivirus TLS interception.
- IPv6-only networks fall back to IPv4 immediately rather than hanging.
- Roblox Studio Team Create works while connected.
- Roblox is reachable on networks that block it at DNS.

### Under the hood

- App version and installer are unified at 3.0.0 with an update gate for old builds.
- Relay tickets are signed per session, and a session now settles its usage on disconnect rather than being estimated.
- Much more small fixes/additions.
