## What's changed

- Reduced background work during play, including repeated process and install scans, optional downloads, and duplicate requests.
- Made connection, game launch, settings and repair operations more responsive by moving blocking Windows work off shared workers.
- Regions now sort by measured ping, with locations awaiting a reading at the bottom.
- Added full-trip estimates for Qatar through Roblox's Doha reference, with consistent Qatar names and flags.
- Kept the last measured relay ping visible while Roblox pauses automatic ping refreshes.
- Improved adapter checks: slow checks offer a retry, and disconnected adapters cannot be recommended.
- Fixed Roblox and VNG client selection so Play respects the chosen installed client.
- Improved game launch tracking and prevented delayed actions from changing a newer game or connection.
- Made per-game settings, global Roblox settings and FastFlags saves work together more reliably, with safer recovery after failed changes.
- Improved overlay visibility, resizing, dragging, FPS sampling and session readings across game and tunnel changes.
- Fixed settings and startup preferences reverting after overlapping saves or resets.
- Improved sign-in timeouts, retry handling and saved-login recovery during temporary service failures.
- Improved Setup, driver repair and update handling, including clearer errors and safer recovery when installation checks fail.
- Reduced unnecessary Lite redraws and background work, and improved settings saves, shutdown and update coordination.
- Improved support logging, diagnostics, notifications and protection against malformed or oversized responses and saved files.
- Removed unused implementation paths and updated affected dependencies.
