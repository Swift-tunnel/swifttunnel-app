## What's changed

Our biggest bug fix update yet, with more than 150 fixes across the whole app.

- Smoother play: much less background work while you game, and the app stays responsive during connecting, launching and repairs.
- Regions now sort by your measured ping, and the last ping stays visible while Roblox is open.
- Added ping estimates for Qatar, with proper Qatar names and flags.
- Fewer connection problems: slow adapter checks offer a retry, and disconnected adapters are never recommended.
- Play now opens the Roblox or VNG client you chose.
- Your settings stick: per-game settings, Roblox settings and FastFlags save reliably, and no longer revert after overlapping saves.

### Everything else

- Improved game launch tracking, so delayed actions can't change a newer game or connection.
- Improved overlay visibility, resizing, dragging, FPS readings and session readings.
- Improved sign-in timeouts, retries and saved-login recovery during temporary service problems.
- Improved Setup, driver repair and updates, with clearer errors and safer recovery.
- Lite uses less background work, with fewer redraws and better settings saves, shutdown and updates.
- Improved support logs, diagnostics and notifications, and better protection against damaged saved files.
- Removed unused code and updated dependencies.
