## What's changed

- SwiftTunnel Lite is now a native app: one process instead of seven, and a fraction of the memory.
- Stuck installs repair themselves in more cases, including a damaged installer copy or a single-user install.
- Country ban bypass works on PCs with no hosts file, instead of reporting a network problem.
- Lite gains a sign-in screen, custom FFlags from a file, an icon, and its own uninstall entry.
- Fixed Lite flickering while typing, and text fields that took no caret or selection.

Release: https://github.com/Swift-tunnel/swifttunnel-app/releases/tag/v3.1.0
